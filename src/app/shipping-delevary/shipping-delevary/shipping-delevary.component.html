Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: March 2026

We strive to deliver your orders as quickly and reliably as possible. Here's everything you need to know about our shipping process.

Delivery Timelines

Standard Orders

Delivered within 7–12 business days across India.

Bulk Orders

Delivered within 10–15 business days depending on quantity and location.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also track your order from the My Orders section of your account.

Delivery Attempts

Our delivery partner will attempt delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the order will be returned to our warehouse and a refund will be initiated after deducting shipping charges.

Damaged or Missing Items

If your order arrives damaged or items are missing, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at info@rapportmart.com with photos of the package and product.

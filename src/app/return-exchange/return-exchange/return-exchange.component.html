Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated: March 2026

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to help with easy returns and exchanges.

Return Window

You may request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving your order. After this period, we are unable to process return requests.

Conditions for Returns

  • The item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original condition with all tags attached.
  • The item must be in its original packaging.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or invoice) is required.

Items Not Eligible for Return

  • Customised or personalised products
  • Innerwear, lingerie, and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
  • Items purchased during clearance or final sale
  • Items that are damaged due to misuse or negligence by the customer

Exchange Process

If you'd like a different size or colour, initiate an exchange from your My Orders page. The replacement will be shipped once we receive and verify the returned item.

Refund for Returns

Once the returned item is received and inspected, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. Shipping charges are non-refundable.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it, provided the order has not been shipped. To cancel, visit your My Orders page or contact our support team.

Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. You may initiate a return after delivery instead.

Refund Eligibility

  • Items must be returned in their original packaging, unworn and with all tags attached.
  • Refund requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery.
  • Customised or personalised products are not eligible for refund.
  • Items marked "Non-Returnable" at the time of purchase are excluded.

Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days. The amount will be credited to your original payment method.

For COD orders, refunds will be transferred to your bank account. You may be asked to provide bank details via email.

Partial Refunds

In certain cases, only partial refunds may be granted — for example, if the item shows signs of use or is not in its original condition.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RAPPORTMART ECOMMERCE PRIVATE LIMITED and its affiliates (collectively "Rapportmart", "we", "our", "us") collect, use, share, protect or otherwise process your personal data through rapportmart.com (the "Platform"). By using the Platform, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. This Policy is published in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Information We Collect

We collect personal data when you register, browse, or transact on our Platform. This includes your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and browsing behaviour. You always have the option to not provide certain information by choosing not to use a particular feature.

We do not collect or store any sensitive payment information such as credit card, debit card, UPI credentials, net banking passwords, or CVV numbers. All payments are processed securely by authorized third-party payment gateways that are PCI-DSS compliant. Your payment details are entered directly on the payment gateway's secure interface and are never accessible to or stored by Rapportmart.

Compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

We process your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). As a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, we are committed to:

  • Processing your personal data only for lawful purposes and with your explicit or implied consent, as applicable.
  • Ensuring accuracy, completeness, and consistency of the personal data we hold.
  • Implementing appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data.
  • Honouring your rights as a Data Principal, including the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data, and the right to nominate a representative to exercise your rights.
  • Not processing personal data beyond what is necessary for the stated purpose.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal, and may limit your ability to use certain features of the Platform.

How We Use Your Data

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To personalise your shopping experience
  • To communicate order updates, offers, and promotions
  • To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized activity
  • To improve our Platform, products, and services

Children and Minors

Our Platform is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from, individuals under the age of 18 years. In accordance with the DPDP Act, 2023, we do not process personal data of children without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us immediately. We will take prompt steps to delete such information from our records. We also do not engage in behavioural monitoring of children or targeted advertising directed at minors.

Sharing Your Information

We may share your data with logistics partners, payment processors, and marketing affiliates to fulfil services. We may also disclose information to comply with legal obligations or protect against fraudulent activity. We do not sell your personal data.

International Data Transfers

Your personal data is primarily stored and processed within India. However, in certain circumstances — such as when using cloud infrastructure, analytics tools, or third-party service providers — your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside India.

Where such transfers occur, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard contractual clauses with the receiving entity ensuring adequate data protection.
  • Transfers only to jurisdictions that provide a level of data protection substantially similar to that under Indian law, or as notified by the Central Government under the DPDP Act, 2023.
  • Your explicit consent, where required under applicable law.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge and consent to such transfers in accordance with this Policy.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

  • Notify the Data Protection Board of India within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act, 2023 and applicable rules.
  • Inform affected users without undue delay, where required by law, with details of the nature of the breach, the data involved, and the steps being taken to address it.
  • Maintain records of all data breaches, including those not required to be reported, for internal audit and compliance purposes.

We take all reasonable steps to contain and remediate breaches promptly. If you suspect any unauthorised access to your account or data, please contact us immediately.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, in accordance with this policy:

  • Account & transaction data – retained for 5 years from the date of your last transaction or account activity.
  • Marketing & browsing data – retained for 3 years from the date of collection, or until you withdraw consent.
  • Legal, tax & compliance records – retained for 7 years as required under applicable Indian laws (including the GST Act and the IT Act).

After the applicable retention period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymised. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us. Certain data may be retained beyond these periods solely where required by law.

Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request information about the personal data we hold about you and how it is being processed.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, or erasure of data no longer needed for its original purpose.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: File a complaint with our Grievance Officer if you believe your data has been processed unlawfully.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your data protection rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer using the details provided below.
